Roxy Vance

"Queen of Sharp Wit"

Roxy Vance has spent 12 years redefining what stand-up comedy can be. Specializing in high-speed dry wit, razor-sharp observations, and fearless storytelling, Roxy's sets leave audiences in stitches from start to finish.

With over 850 live shows performed and 2.1M+ social media followers, her one-hour special "Live & Unfiltered" has sold out 17 consecutive nights at Laughora and is currently in production for a streaming platform release. Why she's popular: Fearless storytelling, razor-sharp timing, and the rare ability to connect with every single person in the room.
