About Laughora

Where stand-up comedy meets an unforgettable night out. Discover the story behind the stage.

OUR STORY

From Open Mic Nights to a Comedy Destination

Laughora was born in 2018 from a simple belief — that live stand-up comedy deserves a stage as vibrant as the performers themselves. What started as a monthly open mic in a rented basement has grown into the city's premier comedy venue, hosting three stages of non-stop laughter every week.

Over 1,200 shows, 450+ registered comedians, and 150,000+ guests later, Laughora is where emerging talents find their voice and headliners test their best material. Our commitment: exceptional sound, intimate sightlines, and comedy that stays with you long after the lights come up.

Our Mission

To create an electric environment where comedy thrives — every seat has a great view, every laugh is heard clearly, and every performer gets the stage they deserve. We believe comedy is a conversation between the stage and the crowd, and we're here to make that conversation unforgettable.

OUR VENUE

Three Stages, One Home

Each space designed for a different comedy experience.

Main Stage

Our flagship room with 200 seats, premium tier options, and state-of-the-art sound. Hosts headline acts, special events, and the monthly Comedy All-Stars Night.

200 seats · Premium tier

Stage B

An intimate 80-seat room where rising comedians hone their craft. Home to our weekly Open Mic Nights and emerging performer showcases.

80 seats · General entry

Full Arena

Our largest configuration at 400+ seats, used exclusively for multi-headliner special events, holiday shows, and big-name touring comedians.

400+ seats · VIP and Premium
THE TEAM

Meet the Founders

The people behind the curtain.

Maya Torres

Co-Founder & Creative Director

A stand-up comedian herself for over a decade, Maya built Laughora to give other comics the stage she wished she'd had. She curates every lineup and coaches emerging talent through our residency program.

Damien Cross

Co-Founder & Venue Director

With 15 years in live event production, Damien ensures every show runs flawlessly — from sound checks to box office. His obsession with acoustics and sightlines makes every seat the best in the house.
