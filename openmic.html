Performance Guidelines

We welcome all kinds of stand-up styles: dark humor, observational, surreal, or musical comedy. Here's what you need to know before stepping on Laughora stage.

  • Strict 5-Minute Sets

    Standard slots are 5 minutes. Feature spots are 7 minutes. No overtime will be tolerated — our timekeeper is ruthless.

  • Original Material Only

    No stolen bits, recycled jokes, or AI-generated scripts. We're here for authentic, original comedy from your brain.

  • Respect the Crowd

    While dark humor is welcome, material targeting specific individuals or groups with malicious intent won't be tolerated on our stage.

  • Free Entry + Cocktail Perk

    Registered comedians get free entry to the show and one complimentary signature cocktail. Arrive 45 min before show time.
